The mark is not a state

The mark has no rule; every state has one. It lives in the Dock, the wordmark and the README, never in the menu bar.

Not the appearance toggle, not the info icon

No circles, no half fills, nothing enclosed. Closest look-alike at 16 px: none/info 83 px.

Measured, not eyeballed

True 1× pixels from headless Chrome. Closest pairs at 16 px: insufficient/none 20, none/stale 24, none/agent 30, unclear/you 30, none/you 36, unclear/agent 36 pixels apart.
