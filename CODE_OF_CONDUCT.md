# Code of Conduct

wasitme adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, by reference. The full text, including the definitions,
the scope and the enforcement guidelines, is here:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

It applies in every space the project uses (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other channel) and when
someone represents the project in public.

## The short version

Be kind and be useful. Welcome people of every background and experience level, give and take feedback gracefully,
assume good faith, and take responsibility when you get something wrong. Keep the focus on the work.

The following will not be tolerated: harassment, personal attacks, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised
attention, trolling, publishing someone's private information (including their session logs, prompts or paths) without
their consent, and anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them. They follow the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) and will respect the privacy and safety of whoever makes a
report.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub's private reporting form:
<https://github.com/draarivpatel-ui/wasitme/security/advisories/new>. The form is made for security reports, but only the
maintainers can read it; start the title with "Conduct:" so it is handled as a conduct report.

All reports are reviewed in good faith and handled confidentially.
